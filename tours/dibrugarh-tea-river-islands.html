Tour Overview

Make a heritage tea bungalow in Dibrugarh your home for five nights and explore the landscapes and living traditions of Upper Assam. Tea estates, village weaving, river islands and Ahom monuments give this journey its gentle rhythm.

Visit Namphake and its Buddhist monastery, look for hoolock gibbons at Barekuri, take a birding boat ride on Maguri Beel, and explore the satras and mask-making traditions of Majuli. A day in Sivasagar and Charaideo brings the region’s royal history into focus.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Dibrugarh

  • Meet at Dibrugarh Airport and transfer to Mancotta or Chowkidinghee Heritage Chang Bungalow for a five-night stay.
  • In the afternoon, take an optional tea walk at Mancotta Tea Estate.
  • Visit Jagannath Temple in the evening.

Overnight: Dibrugarh

Day 2

Tea estates, tasting & village life

  • After breakfast, tour Ethelwold Tea Estate and learn about CTC tea production.
  • Taste different teas with a knowledgeable tea specialist or practising estate manager.
  • Visit Hiloidhari village and enjoy an evening cultural programme at Mancotta Heritage Chang Bungalow.

Overnight: Dibrugarh

Day 3

Namphake, Barekuri & Maguri Beel

  • Drive to Namphake near Naharkatiya and visit the Tai Phake village and its Buddhist monastery. Meet local residents and learn about their traditions and handwoven textiles.
  • Continue to Barekuri village to look for hoolock gibbons.
  • Take a boat ride on Maguri Beel for birdwatching.
  • Return to Dibrugarh and visit Asama in Alimur, where village artisans create handwoven natural-fibre homeware and gifts.

Overnight: Dibrugarh

Day 4

Majuli’s satras & island crafts

  • Travel to Majuli via Bogibeel Bridge, with views of the Brahmaputra; a sunset view at the bridge can be planned if timings allow.
  • Visit Kamalabari, Auniati and Shamaguri satras to explore Assam’s Vaishnavite cultural traditions.
  • Visit a Mishing village, meet traditional mask makers and see the Majuli mustard oil project.
  • Return to the bungalow in Dibrugarh in the evening.

Overnight: Dibrugarh

Day 5

Sivasagar & the royal maidams

  • After breakfast, travel to Sivasagar via Moran. Meet acclaimed weaver Hemprabha Chutia, subject to her availability.
  • An optional visit to Teporcali Bam, known as Hornbill Village, can be arranged.
  • Explore Ahom monuments and temples, including Talatal Ghar, Rang Ghar, Shiva Dol and the Sivasagar tank.
  • Visit the royal maidams at Charaideo before returning to Dibrugarh.

Overnight: Dibrugarh

Day 6

Departure from Dibrugarh

  • Transfer to Dibrugarh Airport in time for your onward flight.

Important Notes

  • Tea factory visits depend on operations. Factories are closed on Mondays; tea plucking and processing demonstrations are unavailable from December to mid-March.
  • November to March is the suggested period in the itinerary for birding at Maguri Beel.
  • Meeting Hemprabha Chutia is subject to her availability; the tea walk and Hornbill Village visit are optional.
  • Wildlife sightings are not guaranteed. Excursion timings, river access and arrangements will be confirmed for your dates.
  • The itinerary also describes organic Mukul Tea Estate, including hand plucking and roasted green tea; ask about adding this experience.
