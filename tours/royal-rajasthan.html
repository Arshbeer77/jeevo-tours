Tour Overview

Step into the world of maharajas, majestic forts, and desert romance with our Royal Rajasthan Odyssey. This luxury journey takes you through India's most regal state, where every city tells a story of valor, romance, and timeless grandeur.

From the Pink City's palaces to the Blue City's fort, from the Lake City's romantic settings to the Golden City's desert magic — experience Rajasthan's royal heritage with stays in heritage hotels, private cultural performances, and exclusive experiences fit for royalty.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Jaipur - The Pink City

Welcome to Rajasthan! Your royal journey begins.

  • Arrive at Jaipur International Airport
  • Traditional welcome with tilak and marigold garland
  • Check-in to heritage palace hotel
  • Evening at leisure to explore hotel grounds
  • Welcome dinner with Rajasthani cuisine

Meals: Dinner | Overnight: Jaipur (Heritage Hotel)

Day 2

Jaipur - City of Palaces

Full day exploring Jaipur's architectural marvels.

  • Morning: Visit Amber Fort - arrive via jeep or elephant (optional)
  • Explore the stunning Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace)
  • Photo stop at Jal Mahal (Water Palace)
  • Afternoon: City Palace - royal residence with museum
  • Jantar Mantar astronomical observatory (UNESCO site)
  • Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds) - iconic photo stop
  • Evening shopping at Johari Bazaar (jewelry & textiles)
  • Optional: Hot air balloon ride at sunrise ($200)

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Jaipur

Day 3

Jaipur to Pushkar (145 km / 3 hours)

Journey to the sacred town of Pushkar.

  • Drive to Pushkar via Ajmer
  • Visit Ajmer Sharif Dargah (Sufi shrine)
  • Check-in to boutique heritage hotel in Pushkar
  • Visit Brahma Temple (one of few in the world)
  • Evening aarti (prayer ceremony) at Pushkar Lake
  • Walk through colorful bazaars
  • Sunset from lakeside ghats

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Pushkar

Day 4

Pushkar to Jodhpur (190 km / 4 hours)

Travel to the Blue City - Jodhpur.

  • Morning visit to rose gardens and local market
  • Drive to Jodhpur through desert landscape
  • Check-in to heritage haveli or palace hotel
  • Afternoon visit to Mehrangarh Fort (one of India's largest)
  • Explore the museum with royal artifacts
  • Walk through the blue-painted old city below the fort
  • Visit Jaswant Thada (white marble memorial)
  • Sunset from fort ramparts with city views

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Jodhpur

Day 5

Jodhpur Exploration

Deeper dive into Jodhpur's culture and crafts.

  • Morning visit to Umaid Bhawan Palace (part museum, part hotel)
  • Clock Tower and Sardar Market for handicrafts
  • Visit to textile printing workshop
  • Bishnoi village safari - meet local artisans
  • See pottery making, weaving, and block printing
  • Interact with Bishnoi community known for nature conservation
  • Evening: Traditional Rajasthani dinner with folk music & dance

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Jodhpur

Day 6

Jodhpur to Udaipur via Ranakpur (265 km / 5 hours)

Scenic drive to the City of Lakes.

  • Depart for Udaipur through Aravalli hills
  • En route stop at Ranakpur Jain Temple
  • Marvel at 1,444 intricately carved marble pillars
  • Continue to Udaipur
  • Check-in to lakeside heritage hotel
  • Evening boat ride on Lake Pichola
  • View City Palace and Jag Mandir from the lake
  • Romantic lakeside dinner

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Udaipur

Day 7

Udaipur - The Venice of the East

Full day in India's most romantic city.

  • Visit City Palace - largest palace complex in Rajasthan
  • Crystal Gallery with world's largest private collection
  • Jagdish Temple (Indo-Aryan architecture)
  • Saheliyon Ki Bari (Garden of Maidens)
  • Visit local miniature painting school
  • Afternoon at leisure or optional spa treatment
  • Evening: Traditional puppet show and cultural performance
  • Rooftop dinner overlooking Lake Pichola

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Udaipur

Day 8

Udaipur to Jaisalmer (550 km / 9 hours or fly 1.5 hours)

Journey to the Golden City in the Thar Desert.

  • Option A: Scenic drive through desert (long but beautiful)
  • Option B: Domestic flight to Jaisalmer (recommended)
  • Check-in to heritage haveli inside the fort
  • Evening walk through the golden sandstone fort
  • Watch sunset from fort ramparts
  • The entire fort glows golden in evening light

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Jaisalmer Fort

Day 9

Jaisalmer - The Golden City

Explore the living fort and desert culture.

  • Morning tour of Jaisalmer Fort - UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Living fort with families still residing inside
  • Visit Jain Temples with intricate marble work
  • Patwon Ki Haveli - five interconnected mansions
  • Salim Singh Ki Haveli and Nathmal Ki Haveli
  • Gadisar Lake with chattris (cenotaphs)
  • Afternoon: Depart for Sam Sand Dunes (45 km)
  • Camel safari into the Thar Desert
  • Luxury desert camp with private tents
  • Sunset over dunes
  • Campfire, folk music, dance under the stars
  • Traditional Rajasthani dinner

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Desert Camp

Day 10

Jaisalmer to Jodhpur (285 km / 5 hours)

Sunrise in the desert, then return journey.

  • Early morning sunrise over sand dunes
  • Breakfast at camp
  • Drive back to Jodhpur
  • Check-in to hotel
  • Afternoon at leisure for shopping or relaxation
  • Optional: Cooking class to learn Rajasthani dishes
  • Farewell dinner at rooftop restaurant with fort views

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Farewell Dinner | Overnight: Jodhpur

Day 11

Jodhpur to Delhi (625 km / 10 hours or fly 1.5 hours)

Return to Delhi for final night.

  • Option A: Overnight train journey (recommended for experience)
  • Option B: Domestic flight to Delhi
  • Arrive Delhi and check-in to hotel
  • Evening free for last-minute shopping
  • Visit Dilli Haat for handicrafts from all states

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Delhi

Day 12

Departure from Delhi

Farewell to the Land of Kings!

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Morning at leisure or optional Delhi sightseeing
  • Check-out and transfer to airport
  • Depart with royal memories of Rajasthan

Meals: Breakfast
