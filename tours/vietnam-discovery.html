10 Days / 9 Nights 5 Cities 2-16 People Year Round

Tour Overview

Discover the enchanting beauty and rich culture of Vietnam on this comprehensive journey from north to south. Experience the bustling streets of Hanoi, cruise through the limestone karsts of Ha Long Bay, wander the lantern-lit streets of ancient Hoi An, and feel the energy of Ho Chi Minh City.

This tour combines UNESCO World Heritage Sites, stunning natural landscapes, delicious cuisine, and warm Vietnamese hospitality. From traditional water puppets to French colonial architecture, from serene rice paddies to vibrant markets — Vietnam will captivate your senses.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Hanoi

Xin chào! Welcome to Vietnam's capital city.

  • Arrive at Noi Bai International Airport
  • Transfer to hotel in Hanoi Old Quarter
  • Check-in and freshen up
  • Evening orientation walk around Hoan Kiem Lake
  • Watch water puppet show - traditional Vietnamese art form
  • Welcome dinner with Vietnamese cuisine introduction

Meals: Dinner | Overnight: Hanoi

Day 2

Hanoi City Tour

Full day exploring Vietnam's cultural and political heart.

  • Morning: Visit Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum and Museum
  • Presidential Palace and Ho Chi Minh's Stilt House
  • One Pillar Pagoda - iconic Buddhist temple
  • Temple of Literature - Vietnam's first university (1070 AD)
  • Afternoon: Hoan Kiem Lake and Ngoc Son Temple
  • Cyclo ride through Old Quarter's 36 streets
  • Each street named after goods traditionally sold there
  • Visit Dong Xuan Market
  • Evening: Street food tour - try pho, bun cha, egg coffee

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Hanoi

Day 3

Hanoi to Ha Long Bay (160 km / 3.5 hours)

Journey to the stunning Ha Long Bay for overnight cruise.

  • Drive through Red River Delta countryside
  • Stop at roadside markets and rice paddies
  • Arrive at Ha Long Bay Marina (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  • Board luxury cruise boat
  • Welcome drink and lunch on deck
  • Cruise through limestone karst formations
  • Visit Sung Sot Cave (Surprise Cave) - stunning stalactites
  • Kayaking or bamboo boat ride in secluded lagoons
  • Sunset from top deck
  • Cooking demonstration on board
  • Dinner with fresh seafood
  • Optional: squid fishing from boat

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Ha Long Bay (Cruise)

Day 4

Ha Long Bay to Hanoi to Hoi An

Morning cruise, then fly to Central Vietnam.

  • Early morning Tai Chi session on deck (optional)
  • Sunrise over Ha Long Bay
  • Breakfast on board
  • Visit floating fishing village
  • Cruise back to harbor
  • Drive back to Hanoi
  • Afternoon flight to Da Nang
  • Transfer to Hoi An Ancient Town (30 km)
  • Check-in to hotel in or near Old Town
  • Evening stroll through lantern-lit streets

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch | Overnight: Hoi An

Day 5

Hoi An Ancient Town

Full day exploring UNESCO World Heritage town.

  • Morning: Walking tour of Ancient Town
  • Japanese Covered Bridge (symbol of Hoi An)
  • Old merchant houses with Chinese architecture
  • Assembly Halls of Chinese congregations
  • Central Market for local crafts
  • Optional: Get custom tailored clothes (Hoi An famous for this)
  • Afternoon: Cooking class - learn to make Vietnamese dishes
  • Market tour with chef
  • Hands-on cooking experience
  • Eat what you cook for lunch
  • Bicycle ride to rice fields and water buffalo
  • Evening: Free time to explore
  • Release paper lanterns on Thu Bon River (traditional custom)

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch | Overnight: Hoi An

Day 6

Hoi An to Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon)

Fly to Vietnam's largest and most vibrant city.

  • Morning at leisure in Hoi An
  • Optional: Visit An Bang Beach (4 km from town)
  • Optional: Visit My Son Sanctuary (Hindu temples, UNESCO site)
  • Transfer to Da Nang Airport
  • Fly to Ho Chi Minh City
  • Check-in to hotel in District 1
  • Evening: Orientation walk
  • Dong Khoi Street for shopping
  • Nguyen Hue Walking Street
  • Dinner at rooftop restaurant with city views

Meals: Breakfast, Dinner | Overnight: Ho Chi Minh City

Day 7

Cu Chi Tunnels & Saigon City Tour

War history and city exploration.

  • Morning: Half-day trip to Cu Chi Tunnels (70 km)
  • Explore 250 km underground tunnel network
  • Used by Viet Cong during Vietnam War
  • See living quarters, hospitals, command centers
  • Opportunity to crawl through tunnels (optional)
  • Afternoon: Return to Saigon for city tour
  • Reunification Palace (former Presidential Palace)
  • War Remnants Museum
  • Notre Dame Cathedral (French colonial architecture)
  • Central Post Office designed by Gustave Eiffel
  • Ben Thanh Market for souvenirs and local snacks
  • Evening: Saigon River dinner cruise with live music

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Ho Chi Minh City

Day 8

Mekong Delta Day Trip

Experience the river life of southern Vietnam.

  • Early departure to Mekong Delta (70 km to My Tho)
  • Board boat to explore Mekong River
  • Visit local islands and fruit orchards
  • Sample tropical fruits (rambutan, dragon fruit, longan)
  • Visit honey bee farm and coconut candy workshop
  • Traditional Vietnamese music performance
  • Narrow canal cruise on sampan boat
  • Experience life along waterways
  • Lunch featuring Mekong specialties (elephant ear fish)
  • Visit floating market if schedule permits
  • Return to Ho Chi Minh City
  • Evening free for shopping or relaxation

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch | Overnight: Ho Chi Minh City

Day 9

Ho Chi Minh City - Free Day

Explore at your own pace or take optional tours.

  • Day at leisure to explore Saigon
  • Optional activities:
  • Visit Jade Emperor Pagoda
  • Explore Cholon (Chinatown) and Binh Tay Market
  • Spa and massage treatments
  • Shopping on Dong Khoi and Le Loi streets
  • Visit Saigon Opera House
  • Take motorbike street food tour
  • Museum visits (Fine Arts, History Museum)
  • Evening: Farewell dinner at traditional Vietnamese restaurant
  • AO show at Saigon Opera House (optional, extra cost)

Meals: Breakfast, Farewell Dinner | Overnight: Ho Chi Minh City

Day 10

Departure from Ho Chi Minh City

Tạm biệt Vietnam! Until we meet again.

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Free time for last-minute shopping
  • Check-out (late check-out available on request)
  • Transfer to Tan Son Nhat International Airport
  • Depart with memories of beautiful Vietnam

Meals: Breakfast
