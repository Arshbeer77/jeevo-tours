Tour Overview

Experience the cultural richness of two neighboring nations on this Delhi & Kathmandu Heritage Tour. Journey from India's bustling capital with its Mughal monuments to Nepal's mystical Kathmandu Valley with its ancient temples, medieval cities, and breathtaking Himalayan backdrop.

This tour combines UNESCO World Heritage Sites, living goddess traditions, vibrant bazaars, and spiritual experiences. From Delhi's Red Fort to Kathmandu's Durbar Squares, from Swayambhunath's monkey temple to Pashupatinath's cremation ghats — discover the incredible heritage of the Indian subcontinent.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Delhi

Namaste! Welcome to India's vibrant capital.

  • Arrive at Indira Gandhi International Airport
  • Meet and greet by our representative
  • Transfer to hotel in central Delhi
  • Check-in and freshen up
  • Evening at leisure to recover from journey
  • Welcome dinner with North Indian cuisine
  • Tour briefing and introduction to the journey ahead

Meals: Dinner | Overnight: Delhi

Day 2

Old & New Delhi Sightseeing

Full day exploring India's capital city.

  • Morning - Old Delhi:
  • Visit Jama Masjid - India's largest mosque
  • Rickshaw ride through Chandni Chowk markets
  • Experience the chaotic charm of old bazaars
  • Visit Red Fort (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  • Mughal architecture and Peacock Throne hall
  • Drive past Raj Ghat (Gandhi's memorial)
  • Afternoon - New Delhi:
  • India Gate war memorial
  • Drive past Parliament House and President's House
  • Qutub Minar (UNESCO World Heritage Site) - tallest brick minaret
  • Humayun's Tomb - inspiration for Taj Mahal
  • Lotus Temple for meditation (if time permits)

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Delhi

Day 3
Day 4

Kathmandu Valley Sightseeing

Explore UNESCO World Heritage Sites of Kathmandu.

  • Morning:
  • Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple)
  • Ancient Buddhist stupa atop a hill
  • Panoramic views of Kathmandu Valley
  • Buddha's eyes watching over the valley
  • Interact with playful monkeys (sacred animals)
  • Kathmandu Durbar Square (UNESCO site)
  • Medieval royal palace complex
  • Kumari Ghar - house of the Living Goddess
  • If lucky, see the Kumari appear at window
  • Ancient temples with intricate wood carvings
  • Afternoon:
  • Pashupatinath Temple - holiest Hindu temple in Nepal
  • On banks of Bagmati River
  • Witness cremation ceremonies (Hindu funeral rites)
  • Observe sadhus (holy men) in meditation
  • Boudhanath Stupa - largest in Nepal
  • Tibetan Buddhist pilgrimage site
  • Walk clockwise around stupa with pilgrims
  • Visit Tibetan monasteries around the stupa

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Kathmandu

Day 5

Bhaktapur & Patan Day Trip

Visit two ancient Newari cities of the valley.

  • Morning - Bhaktapur:
  • Drive to Bhaktapur (14 km, 30 minutes)
  • Best-preserved medieval city in Nepal
  • Bhaktapur Durbar Square (UNESCO site)
  • 55-Window Palace with intricate woodwork
  • Golden Gate entrance
  • Nyatapola Temple - tallest pagoda in Nepal
  • Pottery Square - watch traditional pottery making
  • Try famous Bhaktapur yogurt (juju dhau)
  • Walk through medieval lanes and squares
  • Afternoon - Patan:
  • Drive to Patan (ancient city of artists)
  • Patan Durbar Square (UNESCO site)
  • Krishna Mandir - stone temple masterpiece
  • Golden Temple (Hiranya Varna Mahavira)
  • Buddhist monastery with golden facade
  • Visit Patan Museum - finest in Nepal
  • Explore metal craft workshops
  • Watch artisans creating statues and singing bowls

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Kathmandu

Day 6
Day 7

Departure from Kathmandu

Namaste and safe travels!

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Morning free for last-minute shopping
  • Check-out from hotel
  • Transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport
  • Flight back to Delhi or onward destination
  • Tour ends with memories of two incredible cultures

Meals: Breakfast
