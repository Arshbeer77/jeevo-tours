Tour Overview

Welcome to God's Own Country — Kerala! This enchanting journey takes you through the lush landscapes, tranquil backwaters, aromatic spice plantations, and pristine beaches of South India's most beautiful state.

From the colonial charm of Kochi to the misty tea gardens of Munnar, from the wildlife of Thekkady to the serene houseboats of Alleppey, and finally to the golden beaches of Kovalam — this tour is a complete sensory experience that will rejuvenate your mind, body, and soul.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Kochi (Cochin)

Welcome to Kerala! Arrive at Cochin International Airport and transfer to your hotel.

  • Check-in to hotel in Fort Kochi area
  • Evening at leisure to explore the colonial quarter
  • Optional: Sunset walk along Marine Drive
  • Welcome dinner with traditional Kerala cuisine

Meals: Dinner | Overnight: Kochi

Day 2

Kochi Sightseeing

Full day exploring the historic port city of Kochi.

  • Visit the iconic Chinese Fishing Nets
  • Tour St. Francis Church (oldest European church in India)
  • Santa Cruz Basilica with beautiful murals
  • Dutch Palace (Mattancherry Palace) showcasing Kerala murals
  • Jewish Synagogue in the historic Jew Town
  • Spice market shopping experience
  • Evening: Kathakali dance performance with traditional makeup demonstration

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Kochi

Day 3

Kochi to Munnar (130 km / 4 hours)

Scenic drive to the misty hill station of Munnar.

  • Drive through lush landscapes and waterfalls
  • Stop at Cheeyappara & Valara waterfalls for photos
  • Watch the landscape transform to tea plantations
  • Check-in to resort overlooking tea gardens
  • Evening walk through tea estates
  • Enjoy the cool mountain air (15-25°C)

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Munnar

Day 4

Munnar Exploration

Full day discovering the natural beauty of Munnar.

  • Visit Tata Tea Museum to learn about tea processing
  • Mattupetty Dam and Lake — scenic boat ride
  • Echo Point for panoramic valley views
  • Kundala Lake surrounded by lush hills
  • Visit a working tea plantation with guided walk
  • Tea tasting session
  • Optional: Eravikulam National Park (seasonal, for Nilgiri Tahr spotting)

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Munnar

Day 5

Munnar to Thekkady (90 km / 3 hours)

Journey to Thekkady, home to Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary.

  • Scenic drive through spice-scented mountains
  • Check-in to resort near Periyar Lake
  • Afternoon: Boat safari on Periyar Lake
  • Spot wild elephants, bison, deer, and exotic birds
  • Visit spice plantation (cardamom, pepper, vanilla, cinnamon)
  • Learn about spice cultivation and ayurvedic plants
  • Optional: Martial arts performance (Kalaripayattu)

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Thekkady

Day 6

Thekkady to Alleppey (140 km / 4 hours)

Travel to Alleppey, the Venice of the East.

  • Morning nature walk in Periyar (optional)
  • Drive to Alleppey through Kerala countryside
  • Afternoon: Board your private houseboat
  • Cruise through palm-fringed canals and backwaters
  • Watch village life along the waterways
  • Fresh Kerala seafood lunch prepared onboard
  • Sunset views over the backwaters
  • Overnight on houseboat with all modern amenities

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Houseboat

Day 7

Alleppey to Kovalam (160 km / 4.5 hours)

From backwaters to beaches — journey to Kovalam.

  • Morning cruise and disembark from houseboat
  • Drive to Kovalam beach resort
  • Check-in to beachfront resort
  • Afternoon at leisure on Lighthouse Beach
  • Swimming, sunbathing, or beach walks
  • Watch local fishermen with traditional nets
  • Beachside seafood dinner

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Kovalam

Day 8

Kovalam — Beach & Wellness Day

Relaxation and rejuvenation by the Arabian Sea.

  • Morning at leisure on the beach
  • 10 AM: Traditional Kerala Ayurvedic massage (90 minutes)
  • Full-body treatment with herbal oils
  • Afternoon free for beach activities or pool relaxation
  • Optional: Visit Padmanabhaswamy Temple in Trivandrum (30 min drive)
  • Optional: Surfing or yoga classes available
  • Sunset from Lighthouse viewpoint

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Kovalam

Day 9

Kovalam — Excursion to Varkala

Day trip to the stunning cliff beaches of Varkala.

  • Drive to Varkala (45 minutes)
  • Visit the dramatic cliff-top beaches
  • 2000-year-old Janardhana Swamy Temple
  • Natural mineral water springs (believed to be medicinal)
  • Lunch at cliff-top restaurant with ocean views
  • Shopping for handicrafts and hippie boutiques
  • Return to Kovalam for farewell dinner

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Farewell Dinner | Overnight: Kovalam

Day 10

Departure from Trivandrum

Farewell to God's Own Country!

  • Breakfast at resort
  • Morning at leisure for last beach moments
  • Check-out and transfer to Trivandrum Airport (25 km / 45 min)
  • Depart with memories of Kerala's natural beauty and warm hospitality

Meals: Breakfast
