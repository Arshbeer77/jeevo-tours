Tour Overview

Follow a 15-day route through North and Central India, from Delhi’s lively lanes to Rajasthan’s forts and village traditions, the forests of Ranthambore and the riverfront rituals of Varanasi. This journey combines royal architecture, local crafts, regional flavours and wildlife.

Along the way, explore Neemrana and Shahpura, Jaipur and Sanganer, Chand Baori and Fatehpur Sikri, the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort, Orchha’s palaces and Khajuraho’s carved temples. Train journeys connect Agra with Jhansi and Khajuraho with Varanasi, before a flight returns you to Delhi.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Delhi

  • Meet your representative at Delhi International Airport and transfer to your hotel.
  • Check in and settle in for the start of your journey.

Overnight: Delhi

Day 2

Old Delhi & New Delhi

  • After breakfast, visit Laxminarayan Temple and India Gate. Drive past Parliament House, the Secretariat Buildings and Rashtrapati Bhavan.
  • Explore Qutub Minar, then visit Jama Masjid in Old Delhi.
  • Experience Chandni Chowk and a rickshaw ride. Stop for photographs outside the Red Fort and visit Raj Ghat.

Overnight: Delhi

Day 3

Delhi → Neemrana → Shahpura

  • After breakfast, drive towards Shahpura, stopping at Neemrana Fort Palace in the Aravalli Hills.
  • Explore the restored heritage fort, its terraces and royal architecture.
  • Continue to Shahpura and check in at your hotel.

Overnight: Shahpura

Day 4

Shahpura → Jaipur & Sanganer

  • Enjoy breakfast before travelling to Jaipur and checking in.
  • In the afternoon, visit Sanganer, known for traditional block printing, handmade paper and finely carved Jain temple exteriors. Temple access is restricted.

Overnight: Jaipur

Day 5

Amber Fort & the Pink City

  • After breakfast, explore Amber Fort, including the Diwan-i-Aam, Kali Temple, Jai Mandir and Sukh Niwas.
  • Visit Jaipur’s City Palace and Mubarak Mahal, with its royal textiles, costumes and armoury, and the audience halls.
  • Explore Jantar Mantar and stop for photographs at Hawa Mahal.

Overnight: Jaipur

Day 6

Jaipur → Chokhi Dhani → Ranthambore

  • Travel towards Ranthambore with a visit to Chokhi Dhani.
  • Experience Rajasthan’s village traditions through folk dance, live music, regional cuisine, camel and bullock-cart rides, and the Kalagram handicraft marketplace.
  • Continue to Ranthambore and check in. The visit sequence will be confirmed around travel and opening times.

Overnight: Ranthambore

Day 7

Ranthambore wildlife safaris

  • Head into Ranthambore National Park for morning and afternoon safaris with a naturalist.
  • Look for Bengal tigers, deer, nilgai, wild boar, crocodiles and the park’s varied birdlife. Sightings depend on wildlife movement.
  • Return to the hotel between drives; lunch and dinner are included during the Ranthambore stay.

Overnight: Ranthambore

Day 8

Ranthambore → Abhaneri → Fatehpur Sikri → Agra

  • After breakfast, travel to Agra via Chand Baori at Abhaneri, known for its striking geometric tiers of steps.
  • Explore Fatehpur Sikri, including Jama Masjid, the Tomb of Sheikh Salim Chishti, Panch Mahal, Diwan-i-Khas and the royal palaces.
  • Continue to Agra and check in.

Overnight: Agra

Day 9

Agra’s marble & sandstone masterpieces

  • Visit the Taj Mahal and admire its marble craftsmanship and setting beside the Yamuna.
  • Explore Agra Fort, including its palace and audience-hall complexes, Musamman Burj and Jahangir’s Palace.
  • Visit Itmad-ud-Daulah’s Tomb, often called the Baby Taj, with its marble inlay and lattice screens.

Overnight: Agra

Day 10

Agra → Jhansi by train → Orchha

  • Transfer to Agra Cantt station for the Vande Bharat Express to Jhansi. Meet your representative and continue by road to Orchha.
  • Explore Jahangir Mahal, Raj Mahal and its murals, Ram Raja Temple, Chaturbhuj Temple and Lakshminarayan Temple.
  • Dinner in Orchha is planned in the programme; confirm it in your final meal plan.

Overnight: Orchha

Day 11

Orchha → Khajuraho

  • After breakfast, drive to Khajuraho and check in.
  • Spend the rest of the day relaxing or exploring the surroundings.

Overnight: Khajuraho

Day 12

Khajuraho’s temple artistry

  • Explore the Western and Eastern Groups of Khajuraho temples.
  • Visit Kandariya Mahadev, Chaunsat Yogini, Devi Jagdambe and Chitragupta temples.
  • Discover the Jain and Hindu shrines of the Eastern Group, including Parsvanath, Adinatha, Ghantai and Vamana.

Overnight: Khajuraho

Day 13

Khajuraho → Varanasi by train

  • Transfer to Khajuraho railway station for the Vande Bharat Express to Varanasi.
  • Meet your representative on arrival and transfer to your hotel. Train details will be confirmed for your dates.

Overnight: Varanasi

Day 14

Varanasi at sunrise & Sarnath

  • Take an early-morning boat ride on the Ganges to see the riverfront ghats and daily rituals. Visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple, subject to applicable entry rules.
  • Return for breakfast, then visit Bharat Mata, Durga and Tulsi Manas temples, and view the New Vishwanath Temple at Banaras Hindu University.
  • Explore the BHU campus and museum and the mosque associated with Emperor Aurangzeb.
  • Visit Sarnath’s Dhamekh Stupa, monastery ruins, Ashoka Pillar remains and archaeological museum.
  • Attend the Ganga Aarti ceremony in Varanasi, with timing arranged locally.

Overnight: Varanasi

Day 15

Varanasi → Delhi & onward departure

  • Transfer to Varanasi Airport for your flight to Delhi.
  • On arrival, transfer for your onward international departure. Domestic and international airfares are excluded.
