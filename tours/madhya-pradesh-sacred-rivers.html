Tour Overview

A 21-day cultural journey through Central India, linking the palace towns of Gwalior and Orchha with Khajuraho, Bhopal and the Narmada’s sacred river towns. Delhi, Agra and Varanasi complete a route rich in temple art, historic cities and riverfront traditions, with separate days for Omkareshwar and Maheshwar.

Tour Highlights

Palace towns

Gwalior, Datia, Orchha and Mandu’s hilltop monuments.

Art across the ages

Khajuraho’s temple sculpture and Bhimbetka’s rock shelters.

Sacred river journeys

Varanasi, Ujjain, Omkareshwar and Maheshwar.

Bhopal & Sanchi

Lakes, historic neighbourhoods and Buddhist monuments.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Delhi

Meet your representative at the airport and transfer to your hotel. Rest before the journey begins.

Overnight: Delhi

Day 2

Old Delhi & New Delhi

Visit Laxminarayan Temple, India Gate and Qutub Minar, then explore Jama Masjid and Chandni Chowk. Enjoy a rickshaw ride, a Red Fort exterior stop and Raj Ghat, with a drive past New Delhi’s government buildings.

Overnight: Delhi

Day 3

Delhi → Agra

Drive to Agra and check in. Explore Agra Fort and its Mughal palace architecture.

Overnight: Agra

Day 4

Taj Mahal → Gwalior

Visit the Taj Mahal at sunrise before continuing to Gwalior. Explore Gwalior Fort and the Jai Vilas Palace museum, subject to arrival and opening times.

Overnight: Gwalior

Day 5

Gwalior → Datia → Orchha

Stop at Datia’s palace and temple landscape before continuing to Orchha on the Betwa River. Check in and settle into the medieval town.

Overnight: Orchha

Day 6

Orchha’s palaces & riverfront

Visit Jahangir Mahal, Raja Mahal, the riverfront cenotaphs and Ram Raja Temple. Time permitting, explore the Betwa landscape; optional boating or a sanctuary visit can be discussed with your guide.

Overnight: Orchha

Day 7

Orchha → Khajuraho

Drive to Khajuraho and check into your hotel. Enjoy time to settle in before the following day’s temple visits.

Overnight: Khajuraho

Day 8

Khajuraho’s temple art

Explore the western and eastern temple groups, including Kandariya Mahadeva, Devi Jagadambi, Chitragupta and the Jain temples. Learn about the sculpture and architecture of the Chandela period.

Overnight: Khajuraho

Day 9

Khajuraho → Varanasi

Transfer to the station for your rail journey to Varanasi, with the service confirmed for your travel dates. Meet your representative on arrival and transfer to the hotel.

Overnight: Varanasi

Day 10

Varanasi & the Ganges

Take an early morning boat ride on the Ganges, then explore the ghats and Kashi Vishwanath area where entry is permitted. Visit Bharat Mata and Durga temples and discover the city’s spiritual and craft traditions.

Overnight: Varanasi

Day 11

Varanasi → Bhopal

Transfer to the airport for your flight to Bhopal, with connections as required. Meet your representative and check into the hotel.

Overnight: Bhopal

Day 12

Bhopal & Sanchi

Explore Bhopal’s lakes, Birla Temple and museum, Taj-ul-Masajid, Moti Masjid and historic neighbourhoods. Continue to Sanchi’s Buddhist monuments, with the day’s order arranged around opening times.

Overnight: Bhopal

Day 13

Bhimbetka & Bhojpur

Visit the painted rock shelters at Bhimbetka and the monumental Shiva temple at Bhojpur. Return to Bhopal after a day tracing Central India’s long cultural history.

Overnight: Bhopal

Day 14

Bhopal → Ujjain

Drive to Ujjain and visit Mahakaleshwar Temple where access permits. Explore the city’s sacred setting and Jantar Mantar observatory.

Overnight: Ujjain

Day 15

Ujjain → Indore

Continue to Indore and check in. Enjoy a relaxed introduction to the city.

Overnight: Indore

Day 16

Indore’s royal heritage

Visit Lal Bagh Palace, the city’s palace heritage and Kanch Mandir. Discover the Holkar legacy and the Jain temple’s mirror-work interiors.

Overnight: Indore

Day 17

Indore → Mandu

Drive to Mandu and settle into your accommodation. Take in the hilltop landscape before the following day’s monument visits.

Overnight: Mandu

Day 18

Mandu’s palaces & pavilions

Explore Jahaz Mahal, Hindola Mahal, Hoshang Shah’s Tomb and Jama Masjid. Continue to Baz Bahadur’s Palace and Rani Rupmati’s Pavilion for views across the surrounding landscape.

Overnight: Mandu

Day 19

Omkareshwar excursion

Spend the day exploring Omkareshwar’s sacred island, temples and river setting. A boat ride is arranged subject to local conditions before returning to Mandu.

Overnight: Mandu

Day 20

Maheshwar excursion

Visit Maheshwar’s fort, temples and Narmada ghats. Discover its handwoven Maheshwari textiles and enjoy a river boat ride where conditions permit, then return to Mandu.

Overnight: Mandu

Day 21

Mandu → Indore → Delhi departure

Transfer to Indore airport for the flight to Delhi and your onward journey. Flight connections and a suitable transfer allowance are confirmed before booking.
