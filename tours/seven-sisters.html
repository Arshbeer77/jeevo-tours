Tour Overview

Discover Northeast India through its changing landscapes and the people who call them home. Travel from Meghalaya’s misty escarpments to Arunachal’s forested foothills, Assam’s wildlife grasslands and Nagaland’s village landscapes. Continue to Manipur’s lake country, Mizoram’s hillside capital and Tripura’s royal heritage.

This active 12-day journey brings together experiences from all Seven Sisters: waterfalls and limestone caves, a Kaziranga wildlife safari, village walks, traditional markets, regional food and palace architecture. Local guides introduce the distinct histories and traditions of each destination.

Seven states: Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura.

Travel style: Nature, wildlife, heritage and local culture. Active pace, with early starts and several substantial travel days.

Before You Plan

Dates and regional flights are confirmed on enquiry. This fast-paced programme uses flights between Dimapur–Imphal, Imphal–Aizawl and Aizawl–Agartala, with connections where necessary. A flight requiring an extra overnight may extend the journey.

Manipur is conditional: local conditions, applicable travel advisories and access must be reviewed before confirmation. Check current FCDO advice for UK travellers and the advice applicable to your nationality.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrive in Guwahati → Shillong

Assam & Meghalaya

  • Meet your representative at Guwahati Airport and drive into Meghalaya. Arrival before midday is preferred so the journey can be completed comfortably.
  • Stop at Umiam Lake before continuing to Shillong.
  • Check in and rest. If arrival time permits, take a short evening walk around Police Bazaar and enjoy an introduction to Khasi food.

Overnight: Shillong
Meals proposed: Dinner

Day 2

Sohra’s waterfalls & limestone caves

Meghalaya

  • After breakfast, set out for Sohra, also known as Cherrapunji, with stops at viewpoints along the road.
  • Visit the Nohkalikai Falls viewpoint.
  • Explore Mawsmai Cave, subject to access and your comfort with narrow, uneven passages.
  • Visit the Nohsngithiang, or Seven Sisters, Falls viewpoint, weather permitting.
  • Enjoy lunch locally before returning to Shillong. Waterfall flow and visibility vary by season, so stops may be adjusted.

Overnight: Shillong
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 3

Shillong → Bhalukpong

Meghalaya, Assam & Arunachal Pradesh

  • Leave early for the drive through Assam towards Bhalukpong in Arunachal Pradesh’s foothills.
  • This is a substantial transfer day. Plan regular breaks and keep sightseeing light.
  • Check in at your lodge or hotel on the Arunachal Pradesh side.
  • Enjoy the forested surroundings and Kameng River views, with a short local walk if daylight and conditions permit.

Overnight: Bhalukpong
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 4

Tipi’s orchids → Kaziranga

Arunachal Pradesh & Assam

  • After breakfast, visit the Tipi Orchid Research Centre, subject to opening arrangements.
  • Explore its orchid collections and learn about the region’s plant diversity.
  • Continue through Assam to Kaziranga and check in at your wildlife lodge.
  • Relax before the following morning’s safari.

Overnight: Kaziranga
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 5

Kaziranga safari → Dimapur

Assam & Nagaland

  • Begin with an early jeep safari in Kaziranga National Park, subject to park opening, range access and permit availability.
  • Look for one-horned rhinoceroses, wild buffalo, deer, elephants and birdlife. Wildlife sightings cannot be guaranteed.
  • After breakfast and check-out, continue by road to Dimapur.
  • Keep the remainder of this substantial travel day for the journey and rest; no additional sightseeing is scheduled.

Overnight: Dimapur
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 6

Dimapur → Khonoma → Kohima

Nagaland

  • After an early breakfast, travel towards Kohima and visit Khonoma, an Angami village known for community conservation initiatives.
  • With a local guide, walk through village lanes, learn about Angami traditions and architecture, and view the surrounding cultivated hillsides.
  • Meet residents or craftspeople where visits have been arranged.
  • Continue to Kohima. Visit Kohima War Cemetery if time permits; otherwise, settle into your accommodation.

Overnight: Kohima
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 7

Kohima → Dimapur → Imphal

Nagaland & Manipur

  • Leave Kohima with sufficient time for the road transfer to Dimapur Airport.
  • Fly to Imphal, using a direct service where available. The departure date depends on confirmed flight operations.
  • Meet your local representative and transfer to your hotel.
  • If arrival time permits, visit Ima Keithel, the women-run market, or take a gentle orientation walk. Otherwise, keep the afternoon free.

Overnight: Imphal
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 8

Loktak Lake & Imphal’s heritage

Manipur

  • Subject to local conditions and confirmed access, travel towards Moirang and Loktak Lake.
  • Visit lake viewpoints and observe its distinctive floating vegetation.
  • Visit the INA Memorial in Moirang.
  • Return to Imphal for Kangla, time permitting. The local guide may adjust the sequence. If the lake excursion cannot operate, an approved city programme will be arranged.

Overnight: Imphal
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 9

Imphal → Aizawl

Manipur & Mizoram

  • After breakfast, transfer to Imphal Airport for the journey to Aizawl. Direct services are preferred; a connection may be required.
  • Meet your representative at Lengpui Airport and drive to your hotel.
  • Enjoy an introduction to Aizawl’s hillside setting and a short neighbourhood walk if arrival time permits.
  • Try a Mizo meal, with dietary preferences communicated in advance.

Overnight: Aizawl
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 10

Reiek & Aizawl

Mizoram

  • After breakfast, travel to Reiek for a village and countryside experience.
  • Explore the heritage village area, subject to access, and learn about traditional Mizo homes and ways of life.
  • Enjoy the hill scenery and choose a short walk according to weather and personal ability.
  • Return to Aizawl for a local market or handicraft visit, depending on opening hours.

Overnight: Aizawl
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 11

Aizawl → Agartala

Mizoram & Tripura

  • Transfer to Lengpui Airport for your journey to Agartala. Use a direct flight where available; otherwise, allow the day for a suitable connection.
  • After arrival, check in.
  • If flight timing and museum opening hours permit, visit Ujjayanta Palace and the Tripura State Museum. Otherwise, move the visit to Day 12.

Overnight: Agartala
Meals proposed: Breakfast and dinner

Day 12

Neermahal & departure from Agartala

Tripura

  • With a late-evening departure, leave early for Neermahal, the water palace on Rudrasagar Lake.
  • Visit the palace by boat, subject to boat operations, weather and opening arrangements.
  • Return towards Agartala with ample time for the airport transfer.
  • For an earlier flight, replace Neermahal with a shorter Agartala programme, prioritising Ujjayanta Palace if it was not visited on Day 11.

Overnight: None
Meals proposed: Breakfast
